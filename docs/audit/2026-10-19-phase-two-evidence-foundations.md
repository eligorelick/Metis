## Supported bounded correction: issuer reconciliation presentation

The existing table grouped facts only by duration and ending date, selected the first observation for each metric, and attributed every table to the first fact's source. The provider can retain facts from both a release and CFO commentary. A GAAP EPS from one document and adjusted EPS from another could therefore produce a fictitious common-source bridge; an itemized tax observation could inherit the wrong exhibit citation. FCF presentation also accepted an issuer amount up to three days away from a column and did not require its source to match.

The report renderer now partitions by the complete retained filing reference: form, accession, filing date, exhibit and URL. It calculates an EPS difference only when a known common document, valid reported duration/end date no later than the supplying filing date, established currency, compatible per-share units and unique finite operands support it. Identical observations do not create a conflict; conflicting values remain visible without selecting one for arithmetic. Wrong-unit observations remain separately identified. A finite subtraction must also remain finite after rounding.

The row is labeled **Reported EPS difference (non-GAAP less GAAP)**. It is arithmetic on rounded reported per-share figures, including any share-denominator effects. It is not an item-level tax allocation, an independently admitted adjustment ledger or normalized recurring earning power. Captured tax amounts remain distinct from EPS units. When quarterly trends are absent, the separately displayed issuer basis note keeps its exact text and discloses absent note-level source identity. With quarterly trends present, the existing trend note retains that text without a separate provenance qualification; uniform basis-note provenance qualification is not claimed.

An issuer FCF amount enters a reconciliation column only for the exact same reported period/duration and supplying document, without conflicting observations, and only when the period end is no later than the supplying filing date. Future-period observations keep their raw values and an explicit qualification without derived EPS or joined historical FCF; valid historical columns from the same document remain usable. This is a filing-day chronology guard, not intraday availability attestation. The separately sourced as-reported FCF row remains retained when that join is unavailable. No debt service or capex is deducted again. These rows do not establish complete obligation coverage, discretionary cash or funding capacity.

Persisted report bytes, their original source identifiers and analyst prose are unchanged. The PR37 follow-up expands ingestion beyond the originally reviewed presentation-only scope: newly collected bundles now retain source/value-distinct reconciliation and FCF observations from both release and commentary. Newly computed disclosures and downstream context can therefore contain more observations. Identical mapped observations collapse only within the same complete source, metric/period/value/unit scope. Quarterly issuer EPS/gain selections are withheld on conflicting values, incompatible units or future periods rather than selecting the first retained value or falling back to an older release. Scoring and DCF formulas are unchanged; this does not claim identical newly generated narrative or facts for changed inputs. The shared table builder supplies reader, Markdown and print surfaces. The standalone audit format retains its existing registry/check coverage and does not render these reconciliation tables; this patch does not claim to expand that format's coverage. No provider call, new credential or paid/model fallback is introduced.

## PR37 review correction: retention and reader identity

Two new P2 findings exposed an upstream limit and a reader identity collision after remote CI passed. The provider now collects FCF from both supplying documents and de-duplicates only identical complete observations. The report adapter preserves different source/value/unit observations rather than collapsing a period. A common FCF definition is shown only when the retained currency-compatible observations share that definition; otherwise the report discloses that no common definition is asserted. Per-document raw definitions remain in the input bundle. Separately sourced or conflicting rows are not silently combined.

Source-scoped tables carry an internal key made from complete document identity and table kind. The reader uses this key independently of displayed headings and values, keeping identity stable through reorder or report replacement. Markdown/print headings and source notes retain their presentation; the key is not a new persisted report field.

Regression-first checks cover provider-to-adapter same-period document/value conflicts, identical observations, wrong units and filing chronology, the companion common-definition refusal, and client reader reorder/replacement without duplicate keys. Reader/Markdown/print controls retain both source/value pairs without mutating their input. The earlier candidate and private review packet remain immutable. A fresh exact-commit full gate run, saved-report preservation comparison and independent review are required before updating PR37 or resolving its discussions. No review thread is resolved by this correction record.

# D R Horton report audit and repairs

The completed report's missing company and aspect grades were primarily an
evidence-admission failure. All four analyst/verification stages completed.
The grading code correctly withheld letters below its evidence floor, but the
financial pipeline had rejected corroborated revenue history and could not
establish a usable borrowing-cost basis. Repairing the inputs restores a
provisional overall grade and five of seven aspect grades. Valuation and
leadership retain material evidence limits.

This audit covers saved DHI report 4, its immutable input snapshot, the retained
primary disclosures and the 54-page corrected PDF. It does not replace the
historical report, fetch new market information or commission another analyst
run. Real provider data and diagnostic exports remain local and outside Git.

## Confirmed defects and repairs

| Defect | Cause | Repair and protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue history disappeared from growth, quality and moat calculations | A primary quarterly statement established operating sales, but the issuer policy recognized only narrowly named sales concepts and rejected the corroborated `Revenues` concept across annual history. | Admit a broad concept only from a complete primary operating statement with matching amount, filing, duration and currency. The document must match the fact's filing. Another contradictory sales presentation blocks admission. A sales/other-income bridge with offsetting items cannot establish broad-concept scope. |
| Cost of debt and interest coverage were unusable or misleading | Latest annual interest was zero/undisclosed. Other provider quarters mixed newly capitalized borrowing costs with previously capitalized interest released from inventory into cost of sales. Simply restoring revenue would have produced a false synthetic coverage/rating input. | Use a complete filed capitalized-interest rollforward to refuse misclassified expense cells in both calculations and analyst payloads. Preserve operating EBIT and raw observations. A separately disclosed historical incurred-interest rate requires exact annual duration, currency and same-filing opening/closing debt totals. Select the latest core fact before deciding whether its form is eligible; never revive an older annual copy after a later recast. |
| Annual issuer guidance was missing from the standardized table | The heading parser expected particular quarterly/“expects” phrasing and missed “providing initial guidance” followed by annual bullets. | Bind supported revenue/tax rows to the explicit fiscal-year introduction and exact source spans. Stop at intervening prose or a new outlook; preserve restrictions and refuse partial-year, segment, currency or conflicting-year interpretations. Unsupported metrics remain unparsed evidence. |
| An SEC-announced earnings date was called unconfirmed | The guard required the date and announcement verb in one clause; the issuer used an introductory sentence and quarterly date bullets. The evidence selector also discarded the calendar. | Keep the bounded schedule as one source passage within the existing excerpt budget; recognize consecutive quarter rows only under an affirmative trusted-source introduction. Refuse enclosing negation, tentative status, unrelated dates and conflicting quarter rows. |
| An earnings residual was diagnosed as missing equity-method income | The diagnostic assumed a specific missing operand whenever the direct identity failed. | Describe the reconciliation as unresolved, retaining the numbers and ownership/other-operand limitation. Do not infer an NCI or equity-method amount from the residual. |
| Identical operating-margin limitations appeared repeatedly | The presentation mapped every stored note to a new row. | Deduplicate exact note strings in the reader/export table. Preserve raw notes and distinct dated limitations. |

## Saved-input verification

The original missing-grade outcome was reproduced before editing. Its weighted evidence
coverage was 41.9827%, below the unchanged 50% headline floor. The stored composite
score therefore did not authorize a displayed company grade. Only balance sheet
and technicals met their aspect floors.

Under the repaired method, using the same frozen inputs and Stage B configuration,
headline evidence coverage is 67.4866% and the displayed composite is **D
(neutral), provisional**, with a 52.78 score. The deterministic aspect displays
are fundamentals D, quality C, balance sheet B, moat D and technicals F. Valuation
has 30% applicable evidence and leadership 45.45%; both remain unassessed. These
are versioned house-rule scores, not newly authored analyst judgments.

The admitted historical borrowing-cost rate is 4.0679%, based on the retained
FY2025 incurred-interest amount and matching average borrowing balances. WACC is
9.4588%. The period, filing, denominator and stale-rate limitation accompany the
fallback. Interest coverage and synthetic credit rating remain unavailable;
capitalized interest is never added back to EBIT as financing expense.

Reparsing both the retained release narrative and cached SEC HTML recovers FY2027
revenue guidance of $30–32 billion and an approximately 25.5% tax rate. The source
and header spans match the original text exactly. Home closings, operating cash
flow, repurchases and dividend spending remain unsupported structured guidance
metrics; the narrative evidence is preserved. The actual retained earnings
calendar survives payload selection and confirms the announced January 19, 2027
results date.

An isolated deterministic reconstruction exercises bundle validation, Stage B,
analyst payload assembly, report schema validation, Markdown and print-HTML
exports. It explicitly identifies itself as an audit reconstruction with no new
analyst passes. Its assertions check the real guidance rows, calendar
confirmation, neutral earnings-reconciliation diagnostic, refusal of both
misclassified interest cells and input immutability.

## Remaining limits and issues not demonstrated as defects

- Intrinsic value and scenario targets remain withheld because the retained
  trailing/yearly evidence does not establish the outside shareholders' share of
  consolidated earnings. A book NCI balance cannot price that claim. Parent-common
  cash-flow attribution also limits P/FCF and comparable valuation signals.
- Corporate share-count trends alone do not establish broad stewardship or
  individual executive performance. Compensation and titles do not supply the
  missing performance evidence.
- The raw provider FCF discrepancy was already correctly disclosed. Its positive
  capex sign was added in the provider's reported FCF; standardized cash flow
  correctly deducts capex. No change to the established convention was needed.
- EV differences, peer comparability, share-class scope and report-wide confidence
  caps were qualified in the report. Their evidence gates were retained.
- Citation coverage and numerical matching do not establish whole-report factual
  accuracy. Some narrative claims remain unverified.
- The inspected PDF had no observed clipping, overlapping tables or missing
  text. Its length and table whitespace remain readability limitations.

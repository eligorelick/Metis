payload 1.55.0, Stage B `STAGE_B_2026_10_ACCOUNTING_EVIDENCE_34`.

or a claim that final release acceptance has completed.

## Historical evidence boundary

selected receivables and discontinued-results rows are unavailable. Original
exports remain historical evidence. Neither synthetic fixtures nor the retained
cross-company cohorts recover that missing snapshot. A cohort containing 53
stored reports is not evidence that QCOM report 53 is among them.

Filed total receivables of 4,315M/3,929M and trade receivables of 2,855M/2,347M,
with revenue 44,284M/38,962M, support comparable alternatives for controlled

path. A cross-scope cause of its published ratio remains a hypothesis. Filed
nonzero discontinued results in FY2022/FY2023/FY2024 do not replace the old
two-observation count without its selected rows. The Veoneer program involved
two disposals; the regression showing one disposal across periods is an
illustration of the counting limitation, not a reconstruction of that program.

  separate evidence. An unavailable/data-only label is not a requested model;
  current next-run settings cannot backfill historical execution. Failed
  attempts and their charges stay recorded. A controlled investment-rating
  validator identifier can produce its fixed safe diagnostic. The historical

  No rejected analyst prose is newly exposed by that diagnostic.

  Structural controls retain 15,270M less 8,304M = 6,966M without importing older

## Saved-report read contract

The pure `legacyScopeQualifications` sidecar is headed **Read-time saved-report
clarifications** and is visible near the header, before the affected body, in
classic, dossier, Markdown, print body/HTML and audit Markdown. It is not inserted
into the report schema, recorded manifest, source registry or missing-data rows.
It adds no recorded count, trust numerator/denominator, completeness result,
verification status, grade or report state.

Its new cutoff is the literal 1.53.0. Older reports receive fixed receivables,
lease, Altman-date and discontinued-observation limitations. Missing/malformed
versions receive conservative unrecorded-version wording when the helper is
called; the stored parser still rejects formerly invalid inputs. Current/future
versions receive no new historical migration notes. A narrow exception handles
current assembly from older computed inputs: an exact owned old receivables or
discontinued flag wrapper receives only its fixed scope/interpretation caveat.
The flag itself is not rewritten at current/future versions, and the note never
says the report was built before the cutoff. Ordinary current/future reports,
near matches and analyst prose get no such sidecar. This is necessary because
the data-only builder copies computed flag messages/rules but does not persist
all forensic comparability notes. No current lease matcher or invented saved
scope schema is added. Missing optional collector/timing
metadata nevertheless remains unknown through the existing shared formatters,
regardless of version.

Only two exact historical data-only quality-flag templates may be rewritten:
the receivables-versus-revenue warning at its exact computed source, and the
discontinued-operations warning at its exact computed source. Complete template
and rule-wrapper matching are required. Embedded growth amounts, counts, rule
text, source and severity survive verbatim. The former removes an unsupported
accounting inference; the latter describes fiscal-year observations rather than
recurring portfolio churn. Arbitrary analyst prose and near matches survive
with the separate caveat. Source ID alone never authorizes a rewrite.

Legacy Altman periods, missing-cap refusals, lease boilerplate, DCF operands,
dates and source identities stay recorded. Their exact historical source scope
cannot be reconstructed from a score, rounded ratio or current evidence. This
conservative choice means older wording must be read with the visible caveat;
it is not silently corrected or relabeled newly verified. Task 4's existing
neutral stable captions remain in use. Existing bridge/annualization/share-basis
cutoffs remain 1.50/1.51/1.52, and their composition order is unchanged. The bridge
consumes its old EXCLUDES evidence before the new adapter runs.

Parser-to-reader-to-export repetition is idempotent. The adapter shares untouched
objects and changes only matched flag text; raw stored bytes and original read
modes remain intact. The audit keeps raw labels/attempts/costs and identifies
model labels as recorded, with the same saved-model evidence summary used by
the body. Audit's pre-existing omission of `fairValue.basis` prose remains; this
change does not claim to expose formerly omitted bridge notes or create recorded
evidence. Existing body readers and the parsed report retain those notes.

## Validation and release boundary

Focused offline controls exercise both layouts, direct exports, HTML forwarding,
parser forwarding, real current data-only assembly from controlled older computed
flag inputs, strict/lenient reads, frozen/current/future/malformed versions,
exact/near/analyst templates, escaping, repeat reads, structural sharing and
whole-report preservation outside the two owned text fields. Existing Task 6
and Task 11 controls cover actual source-owned metadata and unknown fallbacks.
No provider/model call, original-run recomputation or historical snapshot repair
is implied by these tests.

Final archived-source cohort replay, every changed-leaf attribution, the named
audit-delta dry-before-write record, independent whole-branch review and exact
final-head release gates are separate acceptance work. Their completion is not
claimed in this source disposition. Published payload restoration history and
timestamp guards remain unchanged.

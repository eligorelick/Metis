## Owner decisions

The owner delegated the calls ("use your recommendation based on research to make this a professional accurate repository"). Each is recorded in D-134; the rules below are stated as the code applies them.

- **OD-1. A period with the non-operating aggregate and no interest element (FIN-507).** Extend B-39: EBIT is pretax income less the `NonoperatingIncomeExpense` aggregate, with a disclosure that no interest element is filed and that an interest line outside the aggregate would leave the figure understating EBIT by that interest. With neither operand the derivation is refused and the withholding names the missing operand; the general route never uses the bank text. Rejected: withholding the quarter (Lilly's DCF falls from $1,079.16 to $842.45 for an operand that cannot change the B-39 result); adding back a zero (invents a value). Revises D-105 (B-39). Sources: SEC Regulation S-X Rule 5-03, captions 7 to 10 (https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/210.5-03); XBRL US rule DQC_0181 (https://xbrl.us/data-rule/dqc_0181).
- **OD-2. A filer-tagged `OtherNonoperatingIncomeExpense` when the aggregate is absent (FIN-509).** Subtract it, as a component of the aggregate (`componentOf: "NonoperatingIncomeExpense"`), so never beside a filed aggregate; name it with its amount; when an interest operand was added back, state the net interest added back as the amount by which EBIT is overstated if the "other" line already holds that interest. The guard is the taxonomy definition plus that disclosed band: companyfacts carries no calculation linkbase, and no independent operating subtotal exists at a filer without `OperatingIncomeLoss`. Rejected: a note only (J&J's EBIT stays 65M); subtracting only a "small" line (no basis in a standard). Revises the three-element adjustment list of commit 63e4fda (no D-entry) and the derived-EBIT wording for an absent aggregate; METHODOLOGY's "removes the non-operating results the filing discloses" now holds. Sources: S-X Rule 5-03 captions 7(d), 8 and 9(b); DQC_0181; eBay's FY2014 10-K R page "Interest and Other, Net (Details)" (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065088/000106508815000054/R82.htm), the interest-inclusive counterexample.
- **OD-3. No stand-in for the keyless one-time-items operand (FIN-509-V1).** When no lookback year has both operands, an info gap says the rule was not evaluated; the narrower "other" element is not used, because it holds interest at some filers (NNN) and not at others. Rejected: the "other" element (false serial-one-time-items flags on interest); pretax less operating income (a new method, with interest-driven breaches at 12 panel filers). Revises RESEARCH "5.3 One-time items" ("Missing operands are unevaluable" now says the gap is disclosed). Sources: RESEARCH 5.3; the eBay R page above; the forensic module's own rule that a check that cannot run emits a disclosed gap.
- **OD-4. Which first-hit synonym is the total (FIN-510, FIN-510-V1, FIN-510-V2).** A `first` step gains component floors (`unlessBelow`, and `unlessBelowTag` for one synonym): a synonym below the same period's value of a listed component, beyond `SYNONYM_FLOOR_TOLERANCE` (the larger of 0.5% of the component and 2,000,000 in the row's currency), is skipped with a note. Floors are declared only where a us-gaap definition puts the component inside the figure: D&A against `Depreciation` and `AmortizationOfIntangibleAssets` (and `DepreciationDepletionAndAmortization` against `DepreciationAndAmortization`, never the reverse); R&D against its excluding-in-process element. With no combined tag that passes and no `Depreciation` filed, D&A is withheld (B-7: amortization alone is never D&A). The FFO stand-in uses the same predicate. A bank's interest income resolves to its operating total first. Rejected: the largest synonym (can pick accretion); a floor at the sum of depreciation and amortization (rounding and scope overlaps reject correct totals); a lower-bound caveat on a figure known to be partial; a floor for HD's sibling deferred-revenue elements. Revises METHODOLOGY "D&A from separate lines (B-7)", the tagSynonyms header, the FFO stand-in's stated error direction (the comment on `GENERIC_IMPAIRMENT_TAGS` in `src/pipeline/stageB/financialMetrics.ts`; D-31 itself is unchanged) and the bank interest-income pin. Sources: the us-gaap definitions of `DepreciationDepletionAndAmortization` (https://sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1547903/000154790321000055/R77.htm), `DepreciationAndAmortization` (https://ir.geegroup.com/annual-reports/xbrl_doc_only/1596), `Depreciation` (https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/quarterly-reports/xbrl_doc_only/4283) and the two R&D elements (https://www.stock-analysis-on.net/Knowledge-Base/Research-and-Development-Expense and https://www.stock-analysis-on.net/Knowledge-Base/Research-and-Development-Expense-Excluding-Acquired-in-Process-Cost).
- **OD-5. Derived fourth quarters and year-to-date differences on one presentation (FIN-511, FIN-511-V2), with C-9.** One helper, `oneBasisPair`, used by the statement builder and the vendor enrichment:
  - A later copy that is a whole multiple of 1,000,000 in a money unit and within 500,000 of the earlier copy only re-rounds it and is not a move.
  - The newest copy of the minuend (the year, or a year to date) counts as re-filed only when it is not the first-reported copy, carries a different value and is not an amendment. A minuend that is not re-filed, or whose subtrahend moved with it (a re-presentation sequenced through a 10-Q and then the 10-K, SEC FRM 13110.4), keeps today's pair, subject to the subtrahend check and rule (b) below; an amended 10-K keeps its corrected figure.
  - A re-filed minuend whose subtrahend did not move with it gives the quarter as first reported only when the re-filing moved it by at most 1% or labels the period a re-presentation: a non-zero discontinued-operations fact (`DISCONTINUED_LABEL_TAGS`) for the period in that filing, new there or moved by more than 1% from its first-reported copy. Otherwise the quarter is withheld, naming both filings and the move, because a corrected figure replaces the original (ASC 250-10-45-23; this agrees with B6 OD-9), and so is a first-reported copy with no subtrahend filed on or before it.
  - The same test runs on every subtrahend of a kept pair against its newest copy filed after the minuend's filing (OD-5 (a2′)); the later copy is still never netted (D-13).
  - A quarter is also withheld when the minuend's filing re-presented earlier periods of the concept (a copy moved by more than 1% from the copy filed just before it; only an amendment is excused for its own year) and a filing that supplies a subtrahend did not (rule (b)).
  - A presentation withholding ends the field: no later synonym, stand-in or derivation of any chain serves it, except the next synonym after a combined synonym the component floor skips as a subset line (final review M-6). That synonym is judged against each floor tag by the filings that carry both, and by its as-filed derived quarter only against a floor tag no filing carries beside it (final re-review N-1). An operand the derived EBIT does not use never refuses it (final review I-1).

  Rejected: netting a later re-presented nine-month copy; keeping the mixed pair for a small re-filing; the first-reported pair for every material re-filing (C-9 replaced it); reading net-income invariance as a label; withholding every quarter whose year was re-filed; the planner's first draft (switch on any re-filing). Revises D-13 ("one filing lineage" now means one presentation) and `src/edgar/statements.ts` invariant 3. Sources: ASC 250-10-45-23 as summarised in PwC Viewpoint 30.7 (https://viewpoint.pwc.com/dt/us/en/pwc/accounting_guides/financial_statement_/financial_statement___18_US/chapter_30_accountin_US/307_correction_of_an_US.html); ASC 205-20-45-3 and 45-3A as reproduced in Deloitte's Roadmap 7.3 (https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/codification/presentation/asc205-20/roadmap-disposals-long-lived-assets-discontinued-operations/chapter-7-presentation-disclosure-requirements-for/7-3-income-statement-presentation-discontinued); the SEC's EDGAR API page (https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), under which companyfacts carries only facts that apply to the entire filing entity; SEC FRM Topic 13, 13110.3, 13110.4 and 13210.1 (https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual/topic-13); D-13.
- **OD-6. Quarterly growth across a re-presentation (FIN-511-V1).** A cell is computed on the basis of the quarter it is printed beside, in three cases: (1) the comparison was re-presented by a filing newer than the printed quarter's own and the printed quarter was not: divide by the comparison's first-reported copy when it was filed on or before the printed quarter's filing and is positive, with a note naming it; otherwise a dash; (2) the printed quarter was re-presented and the comparison has no copy on the new basis: a dash with a note; (3) the printed quarter's filing re-presented earlier periods and the filing supplying the comparison did not (rule (b) on the pair, read from the row's `revenuePresentation` marker): a dash with a note. ## Recorded rules revised without an owner decision

- **FIN-508.** B-39 is keyed on the period, not the tag that served: a period whose interest any filing tags as `InterestExpenseNonoperating` is inside the aggregate and is not added back (DQC_0181). The nesting test counts a quarter of that tag withheld for presentation as tagged.
- **FIN-507-V1.** The derived EBIT's pretax operand reads the same chain as `incomeBeforeTax`: the total, else the B-32 domestic + foreign composition.
- **FIN-510-V2 and FIN-511-V2.** The FFO stand-in shares the builder's floor predicate, and the vendor enrichment shares its pairing helper; the enrichment's texts say "derived".
- **Ruling B (controller, 2026-10-29; revises A-11 for derived values only).** For a derived SEC value, the vendor/SEC allowance is the larger of A-11's allowance and the operands' rounding allowance capped at 1% of the larger figure. The rounding allowance is the sum, over the filed copies the compared figure nets (two, or four for short-term sales plus maturities), of half each copy's rounding unit: the largest power of ten up to 1,000,000 dividing it exactly, 1 for a zero and none for a figure with a fraction. A directly filed value keeps A-11. The income-classification check is not covered (residual list).

## Owner decisions

- **OD-1: a peer market cap is money only in a currency the peer's own data establishes (AI-301, AI-301-V1).** The owner confirmed it on 2026-10-28 with its coverage trade. Sources: FMP, Stock Peer Comparison API (https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/peers), whose "same exchange" premise the stored list for a NASDAQ subject contradicts (`.T`, `.TW`, `.HK`, `.SS` symbols, no currency field); Yahoo Finance, "Exchanges and data providers" (https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN2310.html), for the suffixes; D-28 and D-57.
- **OD-2: insider statistics are counts and share volumes over the trailing four complete calendar quarters (AI-117).** Sources: SEC, "Ownership Form Codes" (https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/ownershipformcodes.html); OpenBB Platform reference for FMP insider statistics (https://docs.openbb.co/odp/python/reference/equity/ownership/insider_trading); Lakonishok and Lee (2001), Review of Financial Studies 14(1) (https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v14y2001i1p79-111.html).
- **OD-3: peer screen reasons travel enveloped; an envelope change alone does not move the payload version (AI-102).** Sources: OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, LLM01 (https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/); D-101.
- **OD-4: a provider's geographic rows are company geography only where the issuer's own XBRL does not contradict them (AI-509).** Sources: Deloitte, Roadmap: Segment Reporting, section 5.5 (https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/codification/presentation/asc280-10/roadmap-segment-reporting/chapter-5-entity-wide-disclosures/5-5-information-about-geographic-areas), quoting ASC 280-10-55-21 and 280-10-50-41; D-80 and D-67 point 3. FMP's Revenue Geographic Segments API page (https://site.financialmodelingprep.com/developer/docs/stable/revenue-geographic-segments) was read through search excerpts only, a weak citation; the decision rests on ASC 280 and D-80.

Calls made without an owner question: AI-505 applies Stage B's house rule (`normalizeQuarterRows`, D-13); AI-507 reads by reporting period, and the 10-K Item 9B read is coverage and is left for later; AI-511 puts the graded window in a note so no registry id or alias group changes; AI-512 withholds a contradicted label, not replaces it (D-66, D-28); residual (a) labels a row only from the date Stage B states and carries the period beside the label so a wrong-year claim cannot validate; AI-310, AI-306, AI-410 and AI-406 name the span, basis or window actually measured.

## Coverage trade and limits

- **Peers (OD-1).** On the entry-tier FMP key most peer profiles are refused and the SEC fallback admits a profile only when the SIC code matches, so U.S. peer caps will often read "(currency unknown)", and a run whose peers are all foreign may show every one that way. The peer table's metrics for such a peer stay empty. The owner chose an honest unknown over a cap in the wrong currency.
- **Provider geography (OD-4).** An issuer that tags its own geography under names the provider does not use (NVDA) loses the provider's annual geography in the AI's input, and stored NVDA reports #47, #48, #50 and #52 show their provider rows unverified. Region-named reportable segments are withheld by design. An issuer whose provider names repeat its own labels (META) keeps them under the caveat note, and stored #44, #45 and #49 are unchanged.
- **Stored AI-101 reports.** See the stored-report table: the figure is shown as stored.

# Reported fiscal payment evidence

## Decision and sources

This is a bounded prerequisite for the funding-bridge recommendation in the [book-to-code disposition](2026-10-17-book-to-code-disposition.md). It extends existing commitment disclosures with optional reported fiscal payment buckets. It does not establish financing capacity or activate a new valuation model.

The source example is [NVIDIA's Q2 FY2027 CFO commentary, EX-99.2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000073/q2fy27cfocommentary.htm), specifically “Commitments,” the two “Future commitments” tables, “Additional Commitments,” and “Guarantees.” The two fiscal tables have separate categories and totals. The guarantee section includes August agreements and has no standalone July snapshot date. Its maximum exposure is a different measure from scheduled payments. The implementation retains those distinctions.

[17 CFR 229.303(b)(1), (i), and (ii)(A)-(B)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229/subpart-229.300/section-229.303) separately addresses contractual cash requirements, internal/external liquidity and anticipated funding sources. Instructions 4, 5 and 8 to paragraph (b) address obligation examples, transfer restrictions and contingent/off-balance-sheet arrangements. A payment table cannot by itself establish these resources or conditions. This is an analytical consistency reference, not a legal compliance opinion.

## Admission and presentation

Records retain original fiscal column labels, table/category identity, printed scale and currency symbol, normalized source-table tokens, reported row totals and supplying filing/exhibit. These are retained text tokens, not byte-identical original HTML. Exact decimal-token arithmetic checks row sums and category totals/columns against the printed table Total. Differences remain visible; reconciliation never repairs a source cell.

“Complete” describes parsing and reconciliation of the supplying table only. It does not establish complete issuer obligations. Unexplained dashes remain unknown, distinct from printed zero. Missing timing is not reconstructed from an aggregate total. The reference tables remain incomplete where dashes have no admitted numeric definition, even when an individual fully numeric category reconciles. Their independently supported totals remain visible.

Admission requires supported table structure, compatible source-period currency, valid dates available by the analysis cutoff and reproduction from retained supplying text. Unsupported or truncated cells, ambiguous category/column boundaries, source/metadata disagreements and future filings cannot establish admitted timing. Agreement with retained text establishes internal consistency, not independent source authentication if both text and metadata were replaced. Independently reproduced aggregate totals can survive damaged optional bucket metadata; their currency must also reproduce.

The flattened-text parser conservatively refuses standalone uppercase three-letter unit-like tokens, attached unit/amount tokens and currency symbols in category spans. A legitimate label such as “CPU capacity” therefore remains unsupported rather than being guessed as a monetary unit or silently dropping one. Raw evidence is retained. Word-led identifiers such as “Tier-1,” “Scope3” and the standard-style “ISO-2027” remain supported; this bounded grammar is not a general filing-table reader.

The report, model payload and exports state coverage and limitations. Only complete, currency-supported, non-Total category buckets enter the numeric payload registry. The pipeline owns this block; it is excluded from the judge output contract. Legacy bundles/reports remain readable without fabricated timing. The disclosed fiscal labels are preserved rather than converted into calendar maturity dates. A table Total is a check, not an additional obligation.

The first printed Total cannot establish table completeness. Conflicting or out-of-order totals and further numeric categories withhold timing. Bounded trailing conditions remain in the timing record and reader outputs; unassigned context or truncated retention prevents complete coverage. Unsupported commentary remains as evidence while an independently supported release table can supply its aggregate. Each record retains its actual exhibit and ordinal within that exhibit, rather than borrowing the preferred exhibit's identity.

Flattened filing text cannot prove that a bare “Guarantees” or “Additional Commitments” phrase is a section heading. Such words inside a cancellation condition remain retained context. Only a complete supported following-table template or explicit guarantee-table caption with units establishes the next table boundary. Ambiguous prose therefore prevents complete parsed-table coverage; numerical reconciliation never establishes complete contractual disclosure.

Malformed optional timing containers are withheld before dereference. An aggregate can survive only when its values and monetary symbol independently reproduce from retained supplying text; missing text does not establish a currency. This is distinct from legacy absence, which retains its existing aggregate-only contract.

## Accounting and method boundaries

Purchases, leases, financing debt and maximum guarantees are distinct measures. Their overlap must be resolved before aggregation. This extension adds nothing to net debt and does not deduct commitments again from enterprise cash flows. Stage B arithmetic, scoring weights, scenario weights and existing valuation eligibility are unchanged.

A future financing bridge still needs dated obligor-level obligations and payment conditions, usable cash and transfer restrictions, committed facilities and draw conditions, covenant definitions, collateral, guarantees, cancellation rights, operating-cash assumptions and debt/lease reconciliation. Missing evidence remains unknown. This feature supplies no solvency conclusion, funding-gap estimate, default probability or investment-return claim.

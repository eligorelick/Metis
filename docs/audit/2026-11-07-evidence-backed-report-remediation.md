Automotive revenue is broader than autonomous-driving chips; platform performance claims alone do not establish incremental handset earnings. These support opportunity coverage, not an independently quantified incremental-profit adjustment.

Future research must distinguish signed agreements, management targets and longer-term possibilities; retain commercial consideration and execution costs; and establish whether consensus already includes them. The remedy is better evidence and transparent sensitivity, not a hardcoded upward target.

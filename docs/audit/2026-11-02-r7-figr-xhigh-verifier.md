## Rejected acceptance proposals

These are deliberate limitations, not claims that every retained sentence is
false. The inventory below distinguishes supported facts from genuine binding
ambiguity/omission and unclear admissible evidence.

- Dropping source content words or using a label from another clause: refused;
  this would let nearby metrics and mixed operating/net-margin rows validate.
- Dropping economic-price approximation or selected-rows/may-overlap qualifiers:
  refused; this would certify a stronger capitalization or aggregate-share claim.
- Promoting an exact point for contingent commitments stated only as an upper
  bound: refused. Recorded obligations are a separate metric.
- Treating all numbers in one cited computed note, or all operands of a growth
  calculation, as proved by one numeric record: refused. H2/consensus arithmetic
  and the sale/purchase comparison need their own evidence.
- Matching the $717/$11,109 benchmark, respectively lists, concentration lists,
  mixed product balances, notional/liability pairs or guidance ranges by number
  presence: refused; direction, metric, unit and period roles could reverse.
- Erasing event, maturity, coupon, Rule 10b5-1 capacity or conditional governance
  dates: refused. Those are separate factual roles, not disposable date noise.
- Inferring missing dash cells or borrowing table headers across omitted spans:
  refused. The source must establish the whole row layout and its dimensions.
- Promoting provider news, roster pay or individual trade notes as filed numeric
  evidence: refused; those notes lack the numeric/excerpt contract required here
  (individual trade prices also do not explicitly declare their currency/unit).
- Borrowing the Item 1 revenue observation for an Item 1A citation, or notes
  maturity for a governance citation: refused; citation identity remains exact.
- Dropping the causal part of the same tax-rate clause, or producer-specific YTD
  operand roles: refused through generic token deletion. Correct underlying
  numbers remain a recorded grammar limit.
- Accepting wrong amounts, periods, units, grades, coverage counts, direction,
  operand order, Form 4 codes, category totals, inclusion/exclusion, a missing
  fiscal basis or a conflicting narrative: refused by paired real-fixture tests.

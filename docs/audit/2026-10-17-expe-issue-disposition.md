## Primary-source method checks

Verified against the [Q2 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324424/000132442426000053/expe-20260630.htm) and [FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1324424/000132442426000008/expe-20251231.htm): outstanding classes differ from issued treasury counts; consolidated equity differs from parent equity; equal per-share common/Class B distributions support an explicit same-price sensitivity; SBC expense and software capitalization are distinct disclosures. These facts support scope/definition repairs, not complete economic valuation.

The [SEC API description](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and [non-GAAP guidance](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations/non-gaap-financial-measures) support publication-aware evidence and separately defined/reconciled non-GAAP measures. [Damodaran's per-share bridge](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/New_Home_Page/littlebook/valuepershare.htm) distinguishes consolidated operating value from outside and nonoperating claims. Those references do not validate arbitrary scoring thresholds or turn book NCI into market NCI.

## Non-negotiable remaining limitations

This PR is a generic reliability repair with a complete disposition inventory, **not closure of all 35 issuer-research tasks**. Primary geography, segment, guidance, FCF and officer bridges now have bounded implementations and actual retained-source checks. Comprehensive normalization/valuation, liquidity funding, legal-response linkage, proxy incentives and realized strategic economics remain open. They must not be reclassified as fixed because parsers pass fixtures. Provider access, publication evidence, source completeness, model variability and unbacktested house rules limit any error-free-report claim.

The [October 7 company announcement distributed by Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/expedia-group-webcast-third-quarter-2026-results-november-4-2026-2026-10-07) supports the earnings-date correction. The [plaintiffs' case page](https://bergermontague.com/expedia/) provides complaint context, not findings of liability or a verified company response.

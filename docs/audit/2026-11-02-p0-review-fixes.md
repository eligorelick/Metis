## Integration review corrections (Claude, 2026-11-02)

The integrating reviewer found three defects in the commits above and corrected them before integration:

- **M-2 clause binding.** The committed splitter treated every comma as a clause boundary, and its dash alternative held two literal question marks instead of em/en dashes (the characters were lost to file encoding). Two consequences: "On 2026-11-04, Figma will report third-quarter results." was no longer qualified, and "Earnings rose 5% year over year — the special dividend is payable 2026-11-04." was qualified. Now sentence ends, semicolons and spaced em/en dashes (written as `\u2014`/`\u2013` escapes) are hard boundaries. A comma splits the clause, but a bare date phrase set off by a comma ("On <date>,", "…on Wednesday, <date>") reads with the piece beside it. Only the comma piece that states the event is amended, so a second use of the same date nearby is left alone. Tests in `tests/report.earningsSchedule.test.ts`: two dash cases and two bare-date-phrase cases were red before the correction; a same-date-twice control was added. All existing boundary cases are unchanged.
- **Verify-pass crash.** The M-2 change read `payload.filings.filter(...)` in `runVerifyPass` and in assembly; a payload without `filings` threw a TypeError (two `tests/stageC.verifyChecks.test.ts` cases failed). The read now falls back to an empty list, matching the neighbouring optional payload reads.
- **Lost characters.** This note's heading and stamp line, and the CHANGELOG heading, carried "?" where the author's dash and arrows were lost to file encoding. Restored, using the CHANGELOG's own heading convention.

Gates after the correction: `tests/report.earningsSchedule.test.ts`, `tests/stageC.payload.passes.test.ts`, `tests/stageC.verifyChecks.test.ts`, `tests/stageC.sectionCheckpoint.test.ts` and `tests/report.forensicObservationContext.test.ts`: 407 passed (receipt `m2b-clauses-green-2`).

- **M-1 scope.** `e786cb36` treated any registrant with more than one SEC ticker as not comparable. That switched off the FIG-A01 protection for FIGR (`FIGR`, `FGRS`), the owner's target issuer, and for registrants whose ticker list carries preferred or note symbols. A registrant with one listed symbol is still compared directly. With several, comparison waits until the vendor's share unit is shown: a comparable no-split period where its weighted-average count (basic or diluted) agrees with the filer's within the existing 3% tolerance. The unit is the vendor's convention for the listing, so in the producer a statement series that shows it also governs the other series. A Berkshire-shaped 1,500-to-1 class unit never agrees and stays uncompared, as do ADRs and unknown listings. Tests in `tests/keyless.shareReconciliation.test.ts`: the multi-ticker comparison, the 1.02x tolerance case and the cross-series case were red before the change (receipts `m1b-red`, `m1b-cross-red`); the 1.04x and unfiled-period controls pass before and after.
- **M-1 on FIGR's retained data.** Offline replay of `tmp/figr-corrected-bundle.json` through the producer (receipt `m1b-producer-replay`, no provider calls). The vendor's Q3 2025 row states EPS 0.21 against the 10-Q's 0.42 basic and 0.34 diluted, with counts exactly twice the filed 103,571,820 basic; it loses all four fields. FY2025 keeps its matching EPS (0.54 basic, 0.44 diluted) but loses counts exactly twice the 10-K's (228,870,518 against 114,435,259 basic; 283,604,000 against 141,802,002 diluted). Under `e786cb36` all six figures would have been published. The 2026 quarters match the 10-Qs and are unchanged; Q4 2025 has no discrete filed fact and stands.
- **Dash escapes.** The M-2 correction's regex and dash tests held literal em/en dash characters although their comments describe escapes; they now hold the Unicode escape sequences the comments describe.

Gates after the M-1 correction: 283 passed across the share, split-basis and keyless consumer suites and the earnings-date tests (receipt `m1b-green-2`); typecheck and lint clean (`m1b-typecheck`, `m1b-lint`).

## Recommendation disposition

### 1. Grade override: confirmed safeguard gap; implement first

The [prompt contract](https://github.com/eligorelick/Metis/blob/65ed563850849269cea7f4f020a4f9412146750b/src/pipeline/stageC/prompts.ts#L512-L516) limits overrides to one letter with evidence. [Assembly](https://github.com/eligorelick/Metis/blob/65ed563850849269cea7f4f020a4f9412146750b/src/pipeline/stageC/passes.ts#L3680-L3747) imports judge sections/verdict and applies confidence/evidence-status safeguards without grade-distance validation. [Evidence withholding](https://github.com/eligorelick/Metis/blob/65ed563850849269cea7f4f020a4f9412146750b/src/report/assessment.ts#L12-L48) already exists and must remain effective on every surface.

Require versioned original/proposed grades, distance, structured rationale and supported citations. Retain the proposed output and mark/reject invalid overrides; do not silently turn A-to-D into a compliant grade. Test A-to-D rejection, unsupported one-step rejection, supported one-step acceptance, strip/section agreement, failed-verification paths and withheld assessment exports. Citation existence is not semantic proof. Book rationale: SA ch. 1, pp. 125–126; Marks ch. 14, pp. 161–169. The one-letter rule is the project's own contract.

Grade order is A, B, C, D, F: D-to-F is one step, not two. A redesign allowing larger risk-driven overrides would require its own explicit methodology change.

### 3. Independently normalized earning power: phased enhancement

Keep reported facts immutable; compute an operating run-rate and an all-in stewardship history separately. Test symmetric gains/losses, reserve charge/reversal neutrality, temporary tax benefits, repeat restructuring and SBC remaining expensed once. Material unknowns may make earning power unestimable.

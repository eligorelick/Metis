# Cross-company eligibility and AFFO safeguards (2026-10-19)

Base: `c3bb13b1c38b214c891d40b1cb6f583c26c1bcd6`. This bounded patch follows an independent offline cross-company audit. It does not establish universal ticker coverage, live provider availability, or predictive rating calibration. No paid model runs, provider subscriptions, shared database changes, deployment or publication are part of this candidate.

## Reproduced defects and policy

1. Current REIT AFFO reconstructed with recurring capex/rent was ranked against historical AFFO using all capex. A controlled NI40 + D&A83 example produced current AFFO113 against historical AFFO103. Historical P/AFFO is now admitted only when its construction is explicitly compatible, independently from FFO. Missing compatibility fails closed. Approximate current AFFO and independently compatible P/FFO remain available. This does not turn a simplified FFO reconstruction into issuer-reported FFO or reconcile JV, preferred and noncontrolling ownership allocations.
2. Missing fund flags admitted an unknown security to company valuation. Company admission now needs explicit equity metadata or, when no explicit type is supplied, both fund flags explicitly false. Explicit unsupported/unrecognized types cannot be erased by false flags; true fund flags override an equity label. A confirmed issuer with an existing incomplete profile can receive only missing public instrument-type metadata, with source, endpoint, observation and retrieval times; vendor fields and envelope remain intact. A fully empty bundle retains its existing source-only fallback. Populated unresolved instruments stop before corporate computation or paid preflight.
3. Explicit Yahoo INDEX, CRYPTOCURRENCY, CURRENCY, FUTURE and OPTION metadata previously became false fund flags. The original type now survives ingestion and produces a supported terminal explanation through stored snapshots and client decoding. Known funds remain rejected, and contradictory equity labels cannot erase true fund flags.
4. Strong financial-business facts conflicting with a label previously left ordinary industrial DCF available. The original route and source observations now remain visible with unresolved model eligibility. Industrial capital/WACC/ROIC/EV valuation and dependent scenarios are withheld; no bank, insurer or REIT model is forced. Controls preserve ordinary companies with vendor financing, fee-only brokers and weak property evidence.

## Primary methodology evidence

- [Damodaran, Valuing Financial Service Firms](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/papers/finfirm09.pdf): financial-company debt and reinvestment have different economic meanings; equity approaches require their own inputs. This supports withholding an unjustified industrial model, rather than choosing a replacement from conflicting evidence.
- [Nareit FFO guidance](https://www.reit.com/nareit/advocacy/policy/nareit-ffo-white-paper-and-related-implementation) and [capital expenditure categories](https://www.reit.com/sites/default/files/media/Portals/0/Files/Nareit/htdocs/policy/accounting/NationalPolicy_%28042601%29.pdf): FFO and capital adjustments require defined treatment. The AFFO comparability gate is this project's conservative implementation policy, not a universal AFFO definition.
- [Realty Income FY2025 supplemental](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/726728/000072672826000009/realtyincomeq42025supple.htm): the existing issuer-derived fixture includes ownership adjustments absent from the simplified reconstruction. It remains an approximate-value limitation and a negative historical-comparability control.
- [SEC ETF bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24) and [SEC ADR bulletin](https://www.sec.gov/investor/alerts/adr-bulletin.pdf): funds and depositary receipts have distinct structures. This candidate rejects funds from issuer DCF while retaining classified equity/ADR admission; it does not claim to resolve every ADR ratio or share class.

## Coverage and limits

The new cross-company suite uses clearly synthetic controlled profiles named for profitable firms, growth/loss-making firms, banks/brokers, insurers, equity/mortgage REITs, utilities, energy/cyclicals, foreign issuers/ADRs and dual-class companies. These labels exercise routing and refusal behavior, not real-company data fidelity. Fund and invalid-symbol controls check admission and syntax separately. Existing deterministic suites cover missing/stale/conflicting evidence, units, fiscal intervals, restatements, split/share basis, cash-flow and debt completeness, provenance and scenario/rating arithmetic. The genuine issuer-derived REIT fixture supplies a separate source-grounded control.

Live issuer runs, provider contracts across exchanges, regulatory capital normalization, complete issuer FFO/AFFO reconciliations, ADR/class ownership reconciliation and out-of-sample rating calibration remain untested here. Funds and explicit non-equity instruments remain unsupported for company analysis. Unclassified instruments remain unresolved. No forced rating distribution, band changes or additional precision is introduced.

Validation results and independent review are recorded in the task evidence and final handoff; this document describes policy, not an assertion that a pending gate passed.

# Evidence-backed report remediation

The audit of a completed semiconductor research run found source omissions, fiscal-period and presentation inconsistencies, and insufficient explanation of growth opportunities. Repair general pipeline behavior, with synthetic regression fixtures and a read-only replay of the frozen input. A lower or higher valuation is not an acceptance criterion.

## Requirements

- Prefer compatible filed financial evidence with explicit provenance; do not erase conflicts, confuse annual and quarterly share denominators, or infer short transition periods from fiscal-date aliases.
- Recover substantive annual notes and complete guidance tables. Retain issuer identity, publication time, accounting basis, scope, and source quality checks. Do not manufacture unavailable governance or issuer documents.
- Treat opportunities as commercial agreements, management targets, or longer-term possibilities according to the evidence. Disclose associated customer consideration, acquisitions, reporting changes, costs, capital needs, and uncertainty. Do not add speculative revenue to consensus or automatically increase fair value.
- Preserve conservative calculation policies unless a general defect is established. Explain tax normalization, incomplete return histories, discretionary buyback capacity, and scenario averaging accurately. Add sensitivities and comparative analysis only with explicit assumptions; never invent segment profitability or target multiples.
- Improve numerical coverage and matching without loosening period, unit, metric, or accounting-basis checks. Provenance is not proof that a claim is true or complete.
- Make displayed units, headings, cross-references and export links correct. Keep research concise without removing material caveats or historical report evidence.
- Historical stored reports and source snapshots are immutable. No real provider data, report exports, databases, or credentials enter the repository.

## Acceptance

Failing synthetic regressions precede fixes. Focused tests, the repository verification gate, frozen-input comparison, and independent review substantiate the final claims. Every audit item receives a disposition, distinguishing implemented defects, supported analytical improvements, and evidence-limited work. The GitHub PR documents actual changes, measured effects and limits.
